# AuctionScope — Content Pillars (the angle machines)

*An **angle is a machine, not a post idea**: a feed + a lens that generates posts indefinitely. Each pillar below names its feed — a pillar without a feed runs dry. Every post any pillar produces multiplies by the three formats (static / carousel / reel) and must pass the copy rules in `copy-playbook.md` (hooks, quality bar, honesty rule).*

## The 8 machines

| # | Pillar | Feed (what powers it forever) | Capacity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deals** | Live inventory, every scrape (price drops, cheapest, closing soon, premium, new) | 5–10 posts/scrape | ✅ automated — this IS the Poster |

## Pillar 2 — Education: the full real-estate syllabus (3 rings)

Not auction-only. The audience for "how do bank auctions work" is people who already found auctions; the audience for real-estate knowledge is **everyone buying property in Tamil Nadu** — education is how they discover us. But general real-estate content is a crowded content-farm graveyard, so we cover it through our lens — **verification and evaluation** — and go deep on **TN specifics** national portals cover badly.

### Ring 1 — Bank-auction core (~25 topics; unique authority)
SARFAESI, step-by-step process (notice → EMD → bid → pay → possession), EMD mechanics and refunds, reserve price vs market value, re-auctions and price cuts, physical vs symbolic possession, "as-is-where-is", how to read a sale notice field by field, auction myths busted, jargon glossary (one term per post).

### Ring 2 — TN property-buying essentials (~40 topics; **the moat**)
- **Documents:** patta, chitta, adangal, EC — and how to pull each on TNREGINET; sale deed and parent documents; FMB sketch; A-register.
- **Approvals:** DTCP vs CMDA, unapproved-plot risks, RERA-TN.
- **Money:** guideline value vs market value, TN stamp duty + registration charges, financing an auction purchase, TDS on property.
- **Land literacy:** cent / ground / ares / sq.ft conversions, UDS in flats, FSI, setbacks.

These are exactly the checks an auction buyer needs anyway — Ring 2 serves both audiences at once, and it's what AI search cites for "patta chitta difference" / "check EC online TN" queries.

### Ring 3 — General real-estate wisdom (~30 topics; broadest reach)
Rent vs buy; auction vs resale vs under-construction; what drives appreciation (metro, highways, flood zones, water table); market cycles and why NPAs create auction supply; road-width rules; facing/vastu basics; negotiation. *(Documented demand: users asked about road widths, facing, and nearby schools in feedback.)*

### Rules for every education post
1. **The anchor rule** — every Ring-2/3 post ties back to evaluation: "this is what to check before you bid; we help you check it." Education is the funnel: Ring 3 attracts → Ring 2 builds TN authority → Ring 1 converts to auction hunters → product.
2. **Honesty rule, education edition** — teach *how to verify*, never certify. "Here's how to pull an EC on TNREGINET," never "this EC is clean." Cite official sources (TNREGINET, TN registration dept, RBI); say "not legal advice" where it matters. All `copy-playbook.md` banned words apply.
3. **Evergreen reuse** — education posts recycle after ~6 months (per the `social` skill's repurposing system); refresh figures (stamp-duty rates etc.) on each reuse.
