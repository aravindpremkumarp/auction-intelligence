# Social & launch kit

Copy and playbooks for launch and ongoing social. Voice: **clear, credible, useful** —
we sell trust, so no hype, no fake scarcity. Show the product doing real work.

Brand assets (logo, wordmark, LinkedIn avatar/banner, colors) live in
[`../../brand/logo/`](../../brand/logo/). Campaign-specific exports go in
[`../assets/`](../assets/).

## The hook

> Bank auctions are where property sells below market — but the deals are buried in
> scanned PDF sale notices. AuctionScope reads every notice and lets you just *ask*.

## Launch posts

### LinkedIn (founder-led, long form)
> For two years I watched below-market property sell at bank auctions while buyers
> struggled — deals scattered across portals, the real details trapped in scanned sale
> notices, no way to compare.
>
> So we built AuctionScope. It reads every Tamil Nadu SARFAESI sale notice (~3,400
> properties and counting), builds a knowledge graph, and lets you ask in plain English:
> "residential auctions in Chennai under 30 lakhs" — and every answer is grounded in the
> actual notice. It never makes up a price.
>
> Paste a WhatsApp forward and it finds the real auction. Ask for a due-diligence workup
> and it runs one. Track a property and see if it gets re-auctioned cheaper.
>
> Live now → auctionscope.in. If you buy auction property in TN, I'd love your feedback.

### X / Twitter (thread opener)
> Indian bank auctions = below-market property. The catch: the details are locked in
> scanned PDF sale notices no one wants to read.
>
> We built an AI that reads all of them. Ask "flats in Chennai under ₹40L" — get grounded
> answers, not hallucinations. 🧵

### WhatsApp / Telegram (one-liner for property groups)
> Found a bank-auction property you're unsure about? Paste the details into
> auctionscope.in — it'll match it to the real auction and pull the sale notice. Free.

## Ongoing content formats

| Format | Cadence | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auction teardown** — "N Chennai auctions under ₹50L this month, analysed" | Weekly | Show the data moat; shareable |
| **Product clip** — 30–60s screen recording of one real query | 2×/week | Demonstrate the "aha" |
| **Educational carousel** — repackaged cornerstone post | Weekly | Top-of-funnel + saves |
| **Re-auction spotlight** — a property that dropped in price | Ad hoc | Unique signal, drives watchlist |

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Landing / hero reviewed and conversion-focused (CTA into chat).
- [ ] Analytics + conversion events live (chat-started, signup, watchlist-add).
- [ ] OG/Twitter cards render correctly (test the share preview — `og-image.png` exists).
- [ ] LinkedIn company page updated with brand assets.
- [ ] 3–5 WhatsApp/Telegram groups identified for seeding (be useful, not spammy).
- [ ] First auction teardown drafted (leads with real graph data).
- [ ] Google Search Console verified; sitemap submitted.
- [ ] Feedback channel obvious in-app (thumbs up/down already flows to the team).

## Guardrails

- **Never invent numbers.** The product doesn't; the marketing can't. Screenshot real
  answers.
- **No legal/investment advice** — link the [disclaimer](../../web/disclaimer.html).
- **No spam.** In WhatsApp/Telegram groups, lead with genuine help; the product sells
  itself when it answers a real question.
