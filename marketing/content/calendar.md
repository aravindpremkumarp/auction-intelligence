# Content calendar

Content serves two jobs: **SEO** (capture searchers early) and **trust** (show the
product doing real analysis). Every post should either rank for a real query or
demonstrate the product — ideally both, via internal links to live facet pages.

## Content pillars

1. **How auctions work** — demystify SARFAESI for first-time buyers (evergreen, SEO).
2. **Due diligence** — how to vet an auction property; where buyers get burned (trust).
3. **Market intelligence** — data-driven teardowns from the graph ("every Chennai
   auction under ₹50L this month") — uniquely ours, highly shareable.
4. **Product-in-action** — walkthroughs of chat, deep-research, paste-a-listing.

## Cornerstone posts (write first — these anchor the SEO mesh)

[`../seo/plan.md`](../seo/plan.md)) and into the app.

## First-quarter cadence (suggested — 1 post/week)

**Month 1 — Foundations (SEO)**
- W1: Cornerstone #1 (SARFAESI process)
- W2: Cornerstone #2 (EMD & bidding)
- W3: Cornerstone #3 (due-diligence checklist)
- W4: Market teardown — "Chennai bank auctions this month, by the numbers" (from graph)

**Month 2 — Trust + product**
- W5: Cornerstone #4 (encumbrance / legal)
- W6: Product walkthrough — "Finding a Chennai flat under ₹40L in 3 questions"
- W7: Cornerstone #5 (re-auctions)
- W8: Market teardown — a second city (Coimbatore / Madurai)

**Month 3 — Depth + distribution**
- W9: Deep-research walkthrough — full due diligence on one real auction
- W10: "NRI's guide to buying Indian bank-auction property remotely"
- W11: Paste-a-listing demo — "Got a WhatsApp forward? Here's how to verify it"
- W12: Quarter recap teardown — trends across TN auctions

## Repurposing (one post → many surfaces)

Each post should fan out:
- **LinkedIn** — a condensed takeaway thread/carousel (founder-led — see [`../social/launch-kit.md`](../social/launch-kit.md)).
- **YouTube** — the walkthroughs and teardowns become short screen-recordings.
- **WhatsApp/Telegram** — a one-line "did you know" + link, dropped into seeded groups.

## Production notes

- Ground every data claim in the product (the app never invents numbers — the content
  shouldn't either). Screenshot real answers.
- Tamil-language variants of the top posts are worth testing for the TN audience.
- Where posts live: decide with the SEO rendering choice (blog on the marketing site vs
  a `/blog` route). Track in [`../seo/plan.md`](../seo/plan.md).
