# Positioning

## One-liner

**AuctionScope finds and vets Indian bank-auction property for you — ask in plain
English, get answers grounded in the actual sale notices.**

## The problem

SARFAESI bank auctions are where distressed property sells below market — but the
market is opaque and hostile to buyers:

- Listings are scattered across bank portals and aggregator sites, in inconsistent
  formats, often as scanned PDF sale notices.
- The signal that matters — reserve price, EMD, exact location, encumbrances, property
  condition, re-auction history — is buried in those notices, not in structured fields.
- Buyers can't easily filter, compare, or spot a genuine below-market deal from a
  re-listed dud.
- Due diligence is manual, slow, and expensive.

## The insight

The data is *public* but *unusable*. The value isn't access — it's **intelligence**:
turning a pile of sale-notice PDFs into a queryable, grounded, comparable dataset.

## What AuctionScope is

An AI intelligence layer over Tamil Nadu SARFAESI auctions (~3,400 properties today,
expanding):

- **Ask in natural language** — "residential auctions in Chennai under 30 lakhs",
  "price range in Kanchipuram?", "borrowers with more than 3 properties". Every answer
  is grounded in a tool call against the knowledge graph — **it never invents prices,
  counts, or IDs.**
- **Semantic search** over the notice text and images — boundaries, neighbourhood,
  legal caveats, condition.
- **Paste-a-listing** — drop a WhatsApp forward or broker blurb and it anchors it to
  the real auction by reserve price + date.
- **Deep research** — a 7-step due-diligence workup on a single auction.
- **Compare + investment reports** — side-by-side and a personalised report.
- **Watchlist + re-auction awareness** — track properties and see price-drop history.

## Who it's for (ICP, in priority order)

1. **Active retail property investors** hunting below-market deals — the core buyer.
   Already looking at auctions, frustrated by the manual grind.
2. **Real-estate brokers / channel partners** who source auction deals for clients —
   power users who'll do volume and refer.
3. **NRIs** buying Indian property remotely — need trust, grounding, and due-diligence
   support they can't get on the ground.
4. **First-time auction buyers** — intimidated by the process; the conversational UI is
   the on-ramp.

## Why we win (differentiators)

| Differentiator | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Grounded answers** — every figure traces to a tool call / source notice | Trust. Auction money is real money; a hallucinated reserve price is a dealbreaker. |
| **Knowledge graph, not a search box** | Enables comparison, aggregates, borrower/bank relationships competitors can't do. |
| **OCR + vision-LLM over the actual sale notices** | The signal lives in the PDFs; we read them so buyers don't have to. |
| **Re-auction awareness** | Distinguishes a real discount from a serial re-list — unique buyer signal. |
| **Conversational + specialised modes** | Lower barrier than portal filters; deeper than a listing site. |

## Positioning statement

> For property investors and brokers who chase India's bank-auction market, AuctionScope
> is the AI intelligence platform that turns scattered SARFAESI sale notices into a
> queryable, verified, comparable dataset you can just talk to — unlike listing
> aggregators that dump raw PDFs and leave the analysis to you.

## What we are NOT (scope guardrails)

- Not a listing aggregator or a lead-gen wall — the intelligence is the product.
- Not legal or investment advice — we surface and structure; the buyer decides (see
  [`../../web/disclaimer.html`](../../web/disclaimer.html)).
- Not (yet) pan-India — Tamil Nadu first, depth over breadth.
