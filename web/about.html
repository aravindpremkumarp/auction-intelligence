About Auctionscope

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Auctionscope is an AI-assisted search and evaluation layer for bank auction properties in Tamil Nadu. We read the public auction listings and their documents, and help a buyer see the things that decide whether a listing is worth bidding on — possession status, encumbrances, extent, and how the reserve compares to local rates — in plain language, before the deadline.

1.What Auctionscope is

Bank auctions in India run under the SARFAESI Act. The listings are public, but the information that matters is spread across a sale notice, an annexure, and sometimes a separate encumbrance certificate — dense documents that are easy to misread under an auction deadline.

Auctionscope brings those listings into one place and puts an AI layer on top. You can search live auctions across Tamil Nadu, open any listing, and ask, in plain language, what its documents actually say — what possession status is stated, whether encumbrances are noted, what the extent and reserve are. The aim is to make the pre-bid homework faster and harder to get wrong, not to make the decision for you.

2.Who we build for

We build for people bidding with their own money: first-time auction buyers, individual investors, and small buyers who do not have a research desk. The common problem is the same — the documents are public but hard to read quickly, and a missed line about possession or an encumbrance can turn a cheap-looking listing into an expensive one.

3.How we work and how we source facts

Two rules shape everything we publish:

  • We do not invent numbers. Reserve prices, extents and dates come from the actual listing documents. Where our guides state a rate — stamp duty, registration charges, a guideline value — we cite the official source (for example TNREGINET) and tell you to confirm the current figure there, because rates change.
  • We separate what a document says from what it means for you. Our tools summarise and surface; the decision, and the legal and title checks behind it, stay with you and the professionals you choose to involve.

Our educational guides and comparison pages are written to the same standard: sourced facts, no invented figures, and a clear line about where our help ends.

4.What Auctionscope is not

Auctionscope is not a law firm, not a title-search service, and not a substitute for verifying a property with the bank. Reading a listing with us is a starting point for your own checks — it does not confirm clear title, it does not clear encumbrances, and it is not legal or financial advice. Before you commit an earnest-money deposit or bid, confirm the details directly with the bank's authorised officer and, where the amount justifies it, with your own advisor. See our Disclaimer for the full terms.

5.Where we operate

Auctionscope focuses on Tamil Nadu — its auction listings, its registration system, and its guideline-value and stamp-duty rules. Keeping the scope to one state is deliberate: the document formats, the local rates, and the ₹/sqft context that make an evaluation useful are all state-specific.
