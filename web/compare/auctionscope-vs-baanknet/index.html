last updated 2026-07-21

They are not either/or: evaluate on AuctionScope, then bid through BAANKNET or the bank's official channel.

What BAANKNET does well

BAANKNET, run under the PSB Alliance, is a government-backed e-auction platform built for India's public sector banks to sell non-performing assets. Unlike a listings-only site, it is a full transaction platform — search to sale — with integrated KYC and secure payment, covering property across India.

  • Official PSB platform — you actually bid here, with integrated KYC and payments
  • Government-backed and national in scope
  • End-to-end: search, register and transact in one place
  • Free to browse

Where BAANKNET isn't built to help

These aren't faults — they're just outside what a listing/auction portal sets out to do:

  • Built for the transaction, not for research — no plain-English search or cross-auction synthesis
  • No evaluation of a property's location, flood risk, connectivity or price-vs-market
  • Focused on public sector bank assets

Where AuctionScope fits

AuctionScope sits a step earlier in the journey — the research and decision. You search Tamil Nadu auctions in plain English, and for any property you can ask the questions a sale notice can't answer: does the area flood, what's nearby, how far is it, and does the reserve look fair against local rates. The answers are researched from the web and cited, and every figure from our own data is grounded, never invented. It does not replace the official portal — you still bid there.

Frequently asked questions

Is AuctionScope an alternative to BAANKNET?

Not a replacement — a complement. BAANKNET is where you find (and, where applicable, bid on) listings; AuctionScope helps you search Tamil Nadu auctions in plain English and evaluate a property's location and price before you bid. Many buyers use both.

Can I bid on a property through AuctionScope instead of BAANKNET?

No. AuctionScope is a search and evaluation tool, not a bidding platform. You place the bid on the official portal; AuctionScope helps you decide which property is worth bidding on.

Does AuctionScope cover the same properties as BAANKNET?

AuctionScope focuses on Tamil Nadu SARFAESI auctions, while BAANKNET is national. For a Tamil Nadu property you may see it in both; AuctionScope adds plain-English search and evaluation on top.

Does AuctionScope do legal or title verification?

No. It researches location, connectivity and market context and surfaces what the sale notice says — it does not do legal or title diligence. Verify the title with the bank and a professional before bidding.
