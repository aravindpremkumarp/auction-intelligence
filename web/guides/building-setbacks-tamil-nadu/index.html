Building setbacks

last updated 2026-07-21

A setback is the open space you must leave between a building and the plot boundary. In Tamil Nadu the front setback runs from about 1.5 to 6 m depending on the road width, side setbacks about 1 to 1.5 m, and the rear setback about 1.5 m. They shape how much of a plot you can actually build on.

What setbacks are for

Setbacks are the required gaps between a building and its plot boundaries, on the front, sides and rear. They exist for light, ventilation, privacy, emergency access and the look of the street, and they are part of what the planning authority checks when approving a building.

The Tamil Nadu requirements

SetbackTypical requirement
FrontAbout 1.5 m to 6 m, depending on the width of the abutting road
SideAbout 1 m to 1.5 m, depending on plot width
RearAbout 1.5 m

These are common figures under the Tamil Nadu Combined Development and Building Rules; the exact requirement varies with plot size, building height and use, so confirm them for the specific plot.

Why they matter to a buyer

Setbacks reduce the footprint you can build on, so the permissible FSI is only achievable after leaving them — the usable build is always smaller than the plot suggests. They also flag risk: a building that has been constructed into its setbacks is a deviation from the approved plan, which can mean regularisation costs, penalties, or trouble with approvals and loans.

The bank-auction angle

For a built property at auction, setback deviations are one of the things an as-is-where-is sale will not warn you about — and they tie back to the occupancy-certificate and approval checks. For a plot, the setbacks decide how much you can actually build. Either way it is a check worth making before you bid. Frequently asked questions

What is a setback in building rules?

The mandatory open space you must leave between a building and its plot boundary — front, sides and rear — for light, ventilation, access and privacy. Planning authorities check setbacks when approving a building.

What are the setback requirements in Tamil Nadu?

Commonly a front setback of about 1.5 to 6 m (by road width), side setbacks of about 1 to 1.5 m, and a rear setback of about 1.5 m — varying with plot size, height and use. Confirm for the specific plot.

Why do setbacks matter when buying?

They reduce the buildable footprint, so the usable build is smaller than the plot suggests. A building constructed into its setbacks is a deviation from the approved plan, which can bring regularisation costs, penalties or loan and approval trouble.
