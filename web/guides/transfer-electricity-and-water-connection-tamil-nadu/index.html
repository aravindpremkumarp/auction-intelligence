Transferring utilities after you buy

last updated 2026-07-21

Once you own a property in Tamil Nadu, transfer the electricity connection (TANGEDCO) and the water/sewer connection (the local body, or Chennai Metrowater in Chennai) into your name, using your sale deed or sale certificate and the patta/tax records. Clear any arrears first — at auction, unpaid utility dues can land on the buyer.

Why transfer the connections

Utilities stay in the previous owner's name until you apply to change them. Transferring the electricity and water connections puts the billing — and the responsibility — in your name, and is usually needed alongside the property-tax and patta transfer for a clean record.

What you need

  • Electricity (TANGEDCO / TNEB). Apply for a name transfer with your sale deed or sale certificate, proof of ownership (patta / tax receipt) and the latest paid bill.
  • Water and sewerage. In Chennai this is the Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (Metrowater); elsewhere it is the local municipality or panchayat. The documents are similar.

The arrears trap — check before you buy

Unpaid electricity or water dues can attach to the service and become the new occupier's problem, and an as-is-where-is auction notice may place them on the buyer. So the time to check utility arrears is before you bid — an unexpectedly large backlog eats into a low reserve. After you buy, clear any dues and complete the transfers.

The bank-auction angle

Winning the auction is not the finish line: patta transfer, property-tax mutation and utility transfers are the steps that actually put the property fully in your name. Building them into your post-purchase plan — and checking arrears up front — avoids surprises. Frequently asked questions

How do I transfer the electricity connection after buying a property?

Apply to TANGEDCO (TNEB) for a name transfer with your sale deed or sale certificate, proof of ownership such as the patta or tax receipt, and the latest paid bill. Confirm the current process on the board's portal.

Do unpaid utility bills pass to the buyer at auction?

They can. Utility arrears attach to the service, and an as-is-where-is auction notice may place them on the buyer — so check electricity and water dues before bidding and clear them after buying.

Who handles water connection transfer in Chennai?

The Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (Metrowater). Outside Chennai it is the local municipality or panchayat. The documents needed are similar to the electricity transfer.
