How the calculation works

  • Base value — the higher of your sale price and the locality's guideline value. The government won't register below the guideline value, so that's the floor.
  • Stamp duty — 7% of the base value.
  • Registration fee — 4% of the base value, or 3% for a woman buyer where the value is below ₹10 lakh (a concession from April 2025).
  • Total — roughly 11% of the base value, a real cost to add on top of the price.

Frequently asked questions

How is stamp duty calculated in Tamil Nadu?

Stamp duty is 7% and the registration fee is 4% of the property value — about 11% together — charged on the higher of your sale value and the government guideline value. So even a low sale price is taxed at least on the guideline value.

What is the stamp duty concession for women in Tamil Nadu?

From April 2025, a woman buyer pays 3% registration instead of 4% where the property value is below ₹10 lakh. Stamp duty stays 7%. Above ₹10 lakh the standard 4% applies. Confirm current eligibility on TNREGINET.

Does guideline value affect my stamp duty?

Yes. The charge is calculated on the higher of the sale value and the guideline value — the government's minimum value for that locality — which you can look up on TNREGINET. If the guideline value is higher than your price, it becomes the base.

Do I pay stamp duty on a bank auction property?

Yes. Stamp duty and registration are payable to register the sale certificate in your name after you win, so budget roughly another tenth of the value on top of your bid.
